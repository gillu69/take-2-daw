Personal Introduction

Hi, I’m Vincent, I’m 19 years old, and I currently live in Dubai. I’m a first-year IT student. Honestly, I wasn’t sure what course to take, but people told me IT would be easy. Now that I’m actually studying it, I’m starting to have my doubts. In my free time, I like to draw, read books, and play guitar. I tend to overthink things, especially when I know I did my best and still wonder why it didn’t seem like enough to some people. Those hobbies help me slow down and clear my head. When I feel like doing something more active, I go to the gym, go for a run, or go swimming. One thing about me is that I really value my peace of mind. I’m the kind of person who would rather protect my peace than try to please everyone. That’s a little about me.

Goals and Aspirations

  • My first goal is to distance myself from people who don’t see my worth. I don’t have to change just to prove that I matter to someone. If I choose to change, I’ll do it for myself and my own well-being. I know who I am better than anyone else does.
  • My second goal is to be there for my grandparents as they grow older, give them the life they’ve always wanted, and help make their dreams come true. I really hope I get the chance to do that because we never know what tomorrow might bring.
  • My third and final goal is to become successful and grow beyond who I am today
