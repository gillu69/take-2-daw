The team behind this website

Who We Are

We are Group 1, a team of five first-year BSIT students. We created Nature’s Canvas as our final project for IT 111 to apply what we are learning about web development and share a little about ourselves. Like different plants in a garden, each member has their own interests, ideas, and goals. This website brings our stories together through our profiles and personal blogs. As we build it, we aim to learn from one another, improve our skills, and create something we can be proud of.

Goals of the Project

1

Our first goal for this website is to make all our members know each other better, through knowing what we like and what we dislike, our personalities, and our hobbies, so that we get to know each other better, moreover, sometimes getting to know each other, often leads to friendship.

2

In this project we aims to bring our ideas and personal stories together in one creative website. It also give us a chance to practice our HTML, CSS and JavaScript skills while learning to share responsibilities and solve problems as a group.

3

We aim to create a website that is easy to explore and understand. With clear menus and readable content, visitors can easily find our profiles and learn more about each member.

4

We aim to use this website to share our experiences and insights as a first year IT student. Through our personal blogs, we can reflect on what we have learned and what we hope to achieve.
